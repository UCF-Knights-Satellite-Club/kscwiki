## Antennas

The band labels (2 m, 70 cm, and 12 cm) describe the radio frequencies the antennas are made for, not their size.

* M2 2M7X — installed 2 m
* M2 425CP16 — installed 70 cm
* DSE1276LY — spare 12 cm loop yagi
