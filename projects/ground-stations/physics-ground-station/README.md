# Physics Ground Station

KSC operates this station with the UCF Department of Physics. Antennas and the rotator sit on the PSB roof. Radios, power supplies, and two computers sit in a lab rack. About 100 ft of cable runs between them.

The club call is [KQ4OHW](registration.md).
